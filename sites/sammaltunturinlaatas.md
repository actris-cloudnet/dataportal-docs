# Sammaltunturinlaatas

Sammaltunturinlaatas is a campaign site in the foothills of Sammaltunturi fell,
Northern Finland. It is one of the three Cloudnet stations operating during the
2026 Pallas Cloud Experiment (PaCE) campaign, alongside
[Pallasjärvi](/site/pallasjarvi) and [Kenttärova](/site/kenttarova).
