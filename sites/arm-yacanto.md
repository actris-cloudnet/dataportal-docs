# Villa Yacanto

The first ARM Mobile Facility (AMF1) operated in Cordoba, Argentina from
September 2018 to April 2019 as part of the Cloud, Aerosol, and Complex Terrain
Interactions Aerial Campaign (CACTI-AC) campaign.
