# Anjalankoski

Anjalankoski (ANJ) is a weather radar site located in Kaipiainen, Anjalankoski,
part of the city of Kouvola, Southeast Finland. The site was established in
1994 as part of the weather radar network of the Finnish Meteorological
Institute (FMI).
