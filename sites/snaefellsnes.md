# Snæfellsnes

Snæfellsnes is a temporary campaign site on the Snæfellsnes peninsula in
western Iceland, operated by [Menapia](https://www.menapia.tech/). They are
hosting the National Centre for Atmospheric Science and the University of Leeds
as part of [GAMB²LE](https://www.gamb2le.co.uk/) research project. The
deployment is an operational trial of the AURORA, a modular, autonomous and
Arctic-hardened observatory developed to measure cloud, precipitation and
boundary-layer processes in remote polar environments.
