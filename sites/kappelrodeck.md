# Kappelrodeck

Kappelrodeck is a cloud remote sensing measurement site located on the
outskirts of the village of Kappelrodeck in the Rhine Valley, at the foot of
the western slopes of the Black Forest in southwestern Germany. The station is
situated at an altitude of approximately 200 m above mean sea level and serves
as the home base of the KLOCX platform operated by the Karlsruhe Institute of
Technology (KIT). Cloud remote sensing observations at Kappelrodeck began in
2025 and contribute to the monitoring and characterization of cloud and
atmospheric processes in a region strongly influenced by both valley and
mountain meteorology.
