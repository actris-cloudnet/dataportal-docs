# Andøya

The first ARM Mobile Facility (AMF1) operated near the town of Andenes on
Andøya island, Norway from December 2019 to May 2020 as part of the Cold-Air
Outbreaks in the Marine Boundary Layer Experiment (COMBLE) campaign.
